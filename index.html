Research focus

What I've been working on, and what I want to work on next.

  • Parameter-efficient adaptation (LoRA, adapters) of large vision and vision-language foundation models to multispectral and hyperspectral satellite imagery.
  • Building world-scale, diverse benchmark datasets for medium-resolution Earth observation, where labeled data is scarce and geographically biased.
  • Dense prediction — semantic segmentation and change detection — from Sentinel-1/2 imagery for buildings, agriculture, and land cover.
  • Turning cloud-native pipelines (Google Earth Engine, PyTorch, GPU serving) into deployed, usable monitoring tools rather than one-off experiments.

Under review · IEEE JSTARS · 2026

S12OpenBuilding: Global Building Footprint Mapping via Efficient Adaptation of a DINOv3 Visual Foundation Model to Sentinel-2 Imagery

Mehdi Golkar Amoli, Mahdi Hasanlou, Danfeng Hong
IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing (submitted)

Introduces S12OpenBuilding, a 111,000-sample, 1,492-region global dataset pairing Sentinel-2 imagery with 10 m building masks, together with a parameter-efficient segmentation pipeline. A lightweight multispectral adapter and LoRA adapt a frozen DINOv3 foundation model to 12-band Sentinel-2 input, reaching a Dice score of 80.37% — a new state of the art over CNN, ViT, and other foundation-model baselines — while training under 5% of the encoder's parameters. The model also degrades far more gracefully than fully fine-tuned CNNs when training data is scarce.
